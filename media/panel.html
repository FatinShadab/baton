Baton routes your coding requests through an intelligent model pipeline:

1
Describe your task: Type a prompt below or click the microphone button to dictate your instruction.
2
Intelligent routing: Gemma 4 (Google AI Studio), Ollama Cloud, or local rules inspect the task and assign a safety risk tier.
3
Handoff or run: Copy context-rich plans to ChatGPT or Gemini, or execute safe tasks directly in your terminal via Codex CLI.

Connect an MCP client

  1. Click Copy config to get the JSON configuration for your client.
  2. Paste it into your client's MCP server configuration (e.g., mcp_config.json).
  3. Restart your MCP client. Baton's local tools and task memory will be available.
